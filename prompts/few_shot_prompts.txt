FEW-SHOT PROMPTING

1. Sentiment Classification
Classify messages as Positive or Negative.

Example 1:
Message: The application is excellent and very easy to use.
Classification: Positive

Example 2:
Message: The application crashes frequently.
Classification: Negative

Now classify:
Message: The application works perfectly and saves me a lot of time.

2. Email Classification
Example 1:
Message: I would like to know the application deadline.
Category: Query

Example 2:
Message: I am unhappy because my certificate has not arrived.
Category: Complaint

Now classify:
Message: Thank you for helping me complete the application.

3. Career Classification
Example 1:
Text: I want to apply for a Python developer role.
Category: Career

Example 2:
Text: Please explain recursion in simple terms.
Category: Education

Now classify:
Text: I am preparing my resume for an AI internship.
